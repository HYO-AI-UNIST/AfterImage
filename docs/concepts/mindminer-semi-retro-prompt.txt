Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept.
Asset type: first art-direction concept sheet for the actual browser board game MINDMINER.
Create a beautifully composed, high-resolution landscape concept design sheet, approximately 3:2. This must feel like a cohesive indie videogame art director's pitch, combining a convincingly playable main game screenshot (about 72% of the sheet, left) and a narrow supporting design column (right). This is a NEW design, not a website mockup. Main theme: semi-retro UI over a clean stylized top-down 3D subterranean mine. Reference the accessible, playful, easily read multiplayer mood of Among Us, while inventing original miner silhouettes and art. No astronauts or recognizable crewmates.

ART DIRECTION:
Friendly underground suspense, chunky toy-like 3D with hand-painted muted colors, low geometric detail, subtly outlined silhouettes, matte rock, slightly worn timber, iron rails and minimal brass. Not photorealistic. Not a pixel-art world. The game WORLD is rendered in 3D, whereas the HUD is 2D semi-retro: strong dark outlines, flat fills, subtly stepped corners, lightly beveled 1990s adventure-game control panels, compact pixel-inspired display lettering and readable typography. No scanline noise across the board. Avoid sleek modern SaaS cards, glass panels, website headers, browser chrome, gradients, purple neon, ornate fantasy frames, and mobile casino styling.

MAIN GAME SCREEN:
A dominant near-vertical orthographic top-down 3D view into a cave mine, enough overhead to make the rail graph instantly legible. A single broad flat mine floor cutaway; gentle height on walls and props. The board is NOT an upright mountain, sloping pyramid, staircase, building or isometric diamond.
Its railway network must be the game's recognizable triangular board: five junction rows with exactly 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 evenly spaced junctions from north to south. 15 junctions total. Each junction connects along its row and to the nearest junctions of the row below to form a clean triangular lattice. Every edge is a SHORT PAIR OF METAL MINECART RAILS on wooden sleepers. Rails must be clear, orderly, straight between junctions, not meandering or a square grid. All fifteen junctions lie on the SAME LEVEL of flat cave floor. Junctions are small circular or hexagonal turntable switch plates. The northernmost single junction connects to a warmly lit EXIT lift portal; the southern five junctions are numbered starting depots 1 2 3 4 5. The board should occupy most of the game screen, with a little breathing room at the edges for rocky cutaway cave walls, timber supports, a few dim cyan mineral crystals, coils of rope and warm caged lamps. Environmental clutter must not obscure the rails or junctions.

One original short stout miner wearing a large yellow safety helmet, tiny headlamp and work overalls rides a simple squat iron minecart on a junction in the lower half. His three immediate legal neighboring destinations have small warm-white selection rings. Do not reveal any hidden mines, colored safe paths, trail history, or other players' private answers. The game is about remembering observed movement. Player colors are warm yellow, muted teal, coral, slate blue, lavender and cream.

Main-screen HUD is restrained and functional, integrated as a game screen. A slim retro status rail at the top: small MINDMINER mark, six small miner portrait badges with one current player outlined amber, and ROUND 02 / 07. No wide sidebar panels. At upper edge inside the game world, a small owner tag reads MIRO'S MINE. At the bottom center, a compact dark chunky retro command bar reads YOUR TURN, followed by three brass hint-token icons and a large clear HINT -1 button. A tiny pause/help control at a corner. Lighting around the current cart is warm amber; only neighboring destinations softly highlight. Cool charcoal rock, desaturated mineral teal, rust iron, warm ivory lettering. Slight irregularity and humor in props, but clear competitive board-game readability.

SUPPORTING DESIGN COLUMN:
On the same dark drafting-sheet background, separated from the hero screenshot by a generous clean gutter, show three deliberate large labeled studies:
1. MINER + CART: a larger original yellow-helmet miner perched in his chunky minecart, three-quarter 3D asset study. Friendly readable face, no visor, no astronaut shape.
2. RAIL JUNCTION: a close-up 3D vignette of the actual rail switch platform with three approaching double rails, a timber sleeper and lamp, showing materials and scale.
3. RETRO HUD: small 2D token and button studies, including an amber selected button and neutral ivory outlined button. Thick 2-pixel-inspired borders, restrained chunky shapes.

At the very top use a clean small title MINDMINER / SEMI-RETRO MINE, and an understated label CONCEPT 01. At the bottom add a tidy six-swatch palette: COAL, STONE, IVORY, AMBER, TEAL, RUST. These supporting labels are outside the in-game HUD. Keep other lettering minimal; no essays or annotations. Crisp purposeful hierarchy, art quality suitable for a real game design presentation. The main game screenshot and supporting studies must feel made by the same team.
